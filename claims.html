How to read this page

• Paid to Provider — the scheme pays the doctor/pharmacy/hospital directly. Most network providers (pharmacies, the dialysis practice, the hospitals) work this way.
• Paid to You — some specialists aren't set up for direct payment, so the scheme reimburses you instead, and you're expected to settle with that provider yourself. In this data that's concentrated in a handful of specialists (Dr Martin Forlee Inc, Dr Fatima Khan Inc, Dr G Picken, and a few others) — almost all tied to one hospital admission.
• Partially Paid — the scheme pays at its own tariff rate, and you're liable for the gap between that and what the provider actually billed. This is the biggest real cost in the data — bigger than rejections.
• Rejected — not one thing. About half of "rejected" R is claims reversed by the supplier (resubmitted, no cost to you). The rest breaks down into real categories below — day-to-day funds being empty is the largest of those.
